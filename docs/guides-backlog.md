# Guides backlog

Purpose: guides exist to earn backlinks and search traffic. Each one answers a real question parents search for, uses public data we can cite, and gives a number worth linking to.

## Planned

- **Bedtime calculator by age and wake time.** Latest bedtime for a given wake time and age, from the AASM consensus sleep ranges (endorsed by the AAP).
- **Snow day odds by city.** Chance of a snow day on school days, from NOAA 1991-2020 climate normals (days with measurable snowfall and snow depth).
- **Hot recess days.** School days a year above 90 F by city, from NOAA normals of daily maximum temperature.
- **Trick-or-treat sunset and weather by city.** Sunset, civil dusk, normal high and low, and chance of rain on October 31, from NOAA solar equations and climate normals.
- **Darkest pickup of the year.** Earliest sunset and the daylight saving time drop in each city, from NOAA solar equations and time zone rules.
