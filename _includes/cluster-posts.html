{%- comment -%} Published posts in one topic hub, newest first. A post is in the hub when its `clusters` front matter has the hub id, or its slug is listed under that hub in _data/clusters.yml. cluster: required hub id. Sets `cluster_posts` for the caller. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cluster_def = site.data.clusters | where: "id", include.cluster | first -%} {%- assign cluster_posts = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for post in site.posts -%} {%- assign post_slug = post.url | remove_first: "/" | split: "/" | first -%} {%- if post.clusters contains include.cluster or cluster_def.posts contains post_slug -%} {%- assign cluster_posts = cluster_posts | push: post -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
