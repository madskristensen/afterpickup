{%- comment -%} Renders one with an AVIF srcset built from the widths CI actually generated. The manifest is written by .github/scripts/resize_images.py, so we never point at a variant that is not on disk. Only two things are ever served: AVIF at several widths, or the plain original (whatever format it was uploaded as) for anything that does not support AVIF or has no manifest entry yet. There is no WebP tier in between; that population is small (AVIF has been Baseline "high" since Jan 2024) and keeping a middle tier only they would use was not worth the extra generated files. Params: src required, e.g. /assets/images/posts/foo.webp alt required, may be empty for decorative thumbnails sizes optional, defaults to the article measure loading optional, "lazy" for below-the-fold images priority optional, "high" for the LCP image {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign img_stem = include.src | split: "/" | last | split: "." | first -%} {%- assign img_base = include.src | split: img_stem | first -%} {%- assign entry = site.data.image_variants[img_stem] -%} {%- assign variants = entry.widths -%} {%- assign img_sizes = include.sizes | default: "(min-width: 40rem) 40rem, 100vw" -%} {%- capture avifset -%} {%- for w in variants -%} {{ img_base | relative_url }}{{ img_stem }}-{{ w }}.avif {{ w }}w{% unless forloop.last %},{% endunless %} {%- endfor -%} {%- endcapture -%} {%- if variants and entry.avif %} {%- endif %}
