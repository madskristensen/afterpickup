{%- comment -%} Renders one with an AVIF srcset built from the widths CI actually generated. The manifest is written by .github/scripts/resize_images.py, so we never point at a variant that is not on disk. Post heroes serve an AVIF srcset, then the original file. Redmond photos serve a WebP srcset at 800, 1200, and the 1600 original. Anything without a manifest entry is a single img. Params: src required, e.g. /assets/images/posts/foo.webp alt required, may be empty for decorative thumbnails sizes optional, defaults to the article measure loading optional, "lazy" for below-the-fold images priority optional, "high" for the LCP image {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign img_stem = include.src | split: "/" | last | split: "." | first -%} {%- assign img_base = include.src | split: img_stem | first -%} {%- assign entry = site.data.image_variants[img_stem] -%} {%- assign variants = entry.widths -%} {%- assign img_sizes = include.sizes | default: "(min-width: 40rem) 40rem, 100vw" -%} {%- assign original_width = variants | last -%} {%- capture avifset -%} {%- for w in variants -%} {{ img_base | relative_url }}{{ img_stem }}-{{ w }}.avif {{ w }}w{% unless forloop.last %},{% endunless %} {%- endfor -%} {%- endcapture -%} {%- capture webpset -%} {%- for w in variants -%} {%- if w == original_width -%} {{ include.src | relative_url }} {{ w }}w{% unless forloop.last %},{% endunless %} {%- else -%} {{ img_base | relative_url }}{{ img_stem }}-{{ w }}.webp {{ w }}w{% unless forloop.last %},{% endunless %} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endcapture -%} {%- if variants and entry.avif -%} {%- else -%} {%- endif -%}
