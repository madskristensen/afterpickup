How many hours can kids play outside after school?

In Redmond, WA, with school out at 3:00 PM and a comfort range of 50 to 85 F, kids get about 412 usable outdoor hours from September to June, around 1 h 54 min a school day. Sunny southern California cities top the list at about 660 hours.

We take the daylight left after school and keep only the share that falls on a typical dry, comfortable day. Change the thresholds above to match your kids.     How we calculate

    For each city and month we work out three things, then multiply them.

    daylight = max(0, average sunset for the month - school end), in hours dry = 1 - (days with 0.01 in or more of precipitation / days in month) comfort = 1 inside [low, high] for the normal daily high, fading in a straight line to 0 at 15°F beyond either edge usable = daylight x dry x comfort (hours per school day) year = sum over Sep to Jun of usable x weekdays in that month

    A few honest limits. These are 30-year averages, not a forecast. A dry day is a day with less than 0.01 inch of precipitation over the whole day, so an afternoon shower on an otherwise sunny day still counts against it. The temperature is the normal daily high, which usually comes in mid afternoon, close to when school lets out. We use each month's average sunset rather than every single day, and we count every weekday from September 2026 through June 2027 with no holidays or breaks removed. Usable hours are a way to compare places and months, not a promise about any one afternoon.

    • Climate: NOAA NCEI U.S. Climate Normals, 1991-2020, monthly station files from ncei.noaa.gov/data/normals-monthly/1991-2020/access/. Fields: MLY-TMAX-NORMAL and MLY-PRCP-AVGNDS-GE001HI. For each city we used the nearest NOAA first-order (WBAN) station with both fields, listed below.
    • Sunset: the NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory solar calculation, with the sun's center 0.833 degrees below the horizon, averaged over each day of the month in 2026 in the city's own time zone, so daylight saving time is included.
    • Cities: the same 51 cities as our Daylight after school guide.

    FAQ

    Which US city has the most outdoor time after school?

    Los Angeles, CA leads our 51 cities with about 665 usable hours from September to June for a 3:00 PM bell, closely followed by Long Beach and San Jose.

    What temperature is too cold for recess?

    There is no single rule. Many schools keep kids inside below about 20 F with wind chill or above about 90 F heat index. Our default comfort range is 50 to 85 F for daily highs, and you can change it.

    Does rain really cut outdoor time that much?

    In wet climates, yes. In Redmond it rains on more than half of the days from November to January, so dark and wet together leave well under an hour a day.

    Is this a forecast?

    No. It uses 30-year NOAA climate normals, so it describes a typical year, not this week.
