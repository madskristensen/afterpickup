Sources and method

The ribbon runs from the end of school to bedtime. Pickup and travel come first, then snack and homework. Dinner sits at the time you choose, and if homework runs into dinner it picks up again right after. Everything else before bedtime is free time. A school week is five times one day.

Sleep: the American Academy of Sleep Medicine consensus statement recommends 9 to 12 hours in 24 hours for children 6 to 12 years, and 8 to 10 hours for teenagers 13 to 18 years, on a regular basis, to promote optimal health (Paruthi et al., Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine, 2016). The American Academy of Pediatrics endorsed those ranges. When you change age or wake time, the suggestion button sets the latest bedtime that still reaches the low end of the range. The ranges are about total sleep, so time spent falling asleep is not counted, and we do not suggest anything they do not say.

Daylight: sunset comes from the NOAA solar calculation for the same 51 cities as our Daylight after school guide, averaged over every day of the chosen month in 2026 in that city's time zone. Free minutes in daylight are the free minutes before that average sunset.
