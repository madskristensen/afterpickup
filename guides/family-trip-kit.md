  - slug: pack-a-foldable-hamper
    heading: A hamper that packs flat
    text: >-
      When we travel, dirty clothes find the floor, because a hotel room
      does not come with a hamper and the suitcase stays open for the clean
      stuff.       We pack a foldable one that opens to full size once the bags
      are down, and we open it before anyone starts living out of a suitcase,
      so we are not hunting under the bed for a sock at checkout.
  - slug: travel-toys
    heading: Toys that stay fresh
    text: >-
      We keep a small set of toys just for trips, Magna-Tiles, Minecraft
      magnetic cubes, figurines, and travel art supplies that do not roll
      around on a tray table. They come out only a handful of times a year,
      which keeps them fresh, and in a new hotel room they give the kids
      something familiar while we unpack or when they wake up early.
  - slug: travel-emergency-kit
    heading: The red case
    text: >-
      The same red first-aid case comes on flights, drives, and cruises,
      already packed with the medicines and small tools we want along, and
      everything inside is TSA-approved so we are not second-guessing it at
      the airport. A few tooth-fairy coins ride in that case too, the same
      Sacagawea dollars we use at home, because a lost tooth does not wait
      until we are back.
  - slug: one-sacagawea-dollar-per-tooth
    heading: A coin for the tooth fairy
    text: >-
      The tooth fairy in our house leaves one Sacagawea dollar for every
      lost tooth, from a roll we keep so we already know where the next coin
      is. We pack a few on trips now, because our oldest once lost a tooth
      while we were away and we had to invent a story that the fairy only
      knew our house.
  - slug: mother-west-wind
    heading: A bedtime book that travels
    text: >-
      We bring a thin Mother West Wind volume on trips so hotel bedtime
      still has a story, the same tales we first found at the cul-de-sac
      lending library. The kids still ask for more, and they fall asleep
      like clockwork at home and in a hotel room.

A hotel room has a way of undoing the small systems that work at home. Dirty clothes find the floor because the suitcase is still open for the clean stuff, and a lost tooth does not care that we are away. Below are the pieces of the trip kit we have written down, the ones that keep those moments from turning into a search.
