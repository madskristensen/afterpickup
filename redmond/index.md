Redmond moves fast once school is back. Between parks programs, council hearings, and one-off city events, it is easy to miss the things that actually matter for a family week.

This is a small proof of concept on After Pickup: a weekly curated digest for Eastside families in Redmond. No email signup yet. Just the archive so we can read a few weeks and decide if it is useful.

Each issue pulls from the city’s weekly bulletin, NewsFlash, the public calendar, parks and recreation listings, and council agendas, then rewrites the useful bits with dates and links.
