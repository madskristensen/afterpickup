## Coming up this week

**Archaeology and Ice Cream**
Monday, Sept. 28, 6:30 to 8 p.m. Family-friendly evening pairing local archaeology with dessert. Confirm venue on the [city calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx) before you go.

**Help shape the new teen center**
The city wants teens (and later the whole community) in the room before a design-build team is chosen. Drop-ins this week and next:

- Wednesday, Sept. 30, 2:30 to 4:30 p.m., Redmond High School Library (teens only)
- Thursday, Oct. 1, 6 to 8 p.m., Redmond Community Center at Marymoor Village (teens only)
- Thursday, Oct. 8, 6 to 8 p.m., Redmond Senior & Community Center, Red Oak Community Room (open to everyone)

Food and hands-on activities are part of the teen sessions. [redmond.gov/TeenCenterProject](https://www.redmond.gov/TeenCenterProject)

**Meet and Green**
Tuesday, Sept. 29, 3 to 4:30 p.m. Environmental sustainability meetup on the city calendar. [Calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx)

**Budget hearing next week**
Tuesday, Oct. 6, 7 p.m. City Council business meeting (2027–2028 budget). Prep links: [Budget engagement](https://www.redmond.gov/BudgetEngagement) · watch [rctvlive](https://www.redmond.gov/rctvlive)

## Looking a little ahead

**Tree giveaway**
Saturday, Oct. 10. Residents up to three trees; smaller local orgs can request more. Application details via city environmental / NewsFlash pages linked from [Parks & Recreation](https://www.redmond.gov/165/Parks-Recreation).

**Salary Commission applications**
Still open for 98052 residents; residency verification due Oct. 16. [Salary Commission](https://www.redmond.gov/SalaryCommission)
