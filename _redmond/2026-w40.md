The week to plan around is the teen center drop-ins. Two are for teens only, and the city is also putting a family history night and a Saturday breakfast on the calendar.

## Teen center drop-ins

Teens ages 12 to 18, or anyone currently in middle or high school, can drop in, get food, try a few hands-on activities, and say what they want the future teen center to feel like. The city is not collecting names, and these sessions are not a design vote.

- Wednesday, Sept. 30, 2:30 to 4:30 p.m., Redmond High School Library. [Calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3552).
- Thursday, Oct. 1, 6 to 8 p.m., Redmond Community Center at Marymoor Village. [Calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3553).

The project page is [redmond.gov/2494](https://www.redmond.gov/2494/Teen-Center-Project). The [Sept. 15 news release](https://www.redmond.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/3068) has the same times.

The community session is the following week: Thursday, Oct. 8, 6 to 8 p.m., Red Oak Community Room at the Redmond Senior and Community Center. It is open to everyone, and the city says free childcare will be available for ages 3 to 12. [Calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3554).

## Archaeology and ice cream

Monday, Sept. 28, 6:30 to 8 p.m. at Redmond City Hall, 15670 NE 85th Street. The Snoqualmie Indian Tribe and the city will talk about protecting cultural and natural resources, archaeology, and the relationship between the two governments. The city calls it family-friendly and asks for an RSVP to help with planning. [Event page](https://www.redmond.gov/1354/Significance-of-the-Bear-Creek-Site) and [calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3550).

## Meet and Green

Tuesday, Sept. 29, drop in anytime from 3 to 4:30 p.m. at Redmond Library. The sustainability team hosts this monthly hour for questions about climate work in Redmond. [Calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3551).

## Flapjacks and Flannel

Saturday, Oct. 3, 9:30 a.m. to noon at the Redmond Senior and Community Center. The calendar lists the 2nd Annual Flapjacks and Flannel Breakfast as a community event. [Calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3538).

## Just after this week

- The next 2027-2028 budget hearing is Tuesday, Oct. 6, at 7 p.m., during the City Council business meeting. Hearings are also set for Oct. 20 and Nov. 2. [Budget engagement](https://www.redmond.gov/2622/Budget-Engagement).
- Tree pickup for people who already applied is Saturday, Oct. 10, at the municipal campus parking lot off 160th Avenue NE. The application is closed. [Tree Giveaway Program](https://www.redmond.gov/2233/Tree-Giveaway-Program).
