A welcoming, arts-filled week in Redmond: Art Walk Thursday, market and park concerts Saturday, and easy library and culture outings for the whole crew.

## Welcoming Week wrap
City and partners celebrate belonging through **Sep 19**. Theme: Stories We Share: The Courage to Connect.  
[Welcoming Week](https://www.redmond.gov/1254/Welcoming-Week)

**Stories We Share** · Wed Sep 16 · 5:00–7:00 p.m.  
The Together Center, 16305 NE 87th St  
[Details](https://www.redmond.gov/1254/Welcoming-Week)

## Downtown Redmond Art Walk
**Thu Sep 17 · 4:00–7:00 p.m.**  
Downtown Park (16101 Redmond Way) and participating downtown spots  
Free art activations, a downtown map, poetry with Poet Laureate Nancy Mburu (4:45–5:30 p.m. on Buoyant Pavilion), then Salsa in the Park (5:45–6:45 p.m.).  
[Art Walk](https://www.redmond.gov/1967/Downtown-Redmond-Art-Walk) · [Park artists & schedule](https://www.redmond.gov/2044/Downtown-Park-Artists)

## Markets & park evenings
**Redmond Saturday Market** · Sat Sep 19 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.  
9900 Willows Rd NE · Produce, treats, crafts, live music through Oct 31.  
[redmondsaturdaymarket.org](http://www.redmondsaturdaymarket.org/)

**Arts in the Park** · Sat Sep 19 · 4:00–8:00 p.m.  
Downtown Park · Free concerts plus Eastside Made artisan pop-up. Lineup: Redmond Academy of Theatre Arts, Seattle Community Theater.  
[OneRedmond](https://engage.oneredmond.org/events/arts-in-the-park) · [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/arts-in-the-park-redmond-tickets-1996026726671)

## Culture & community

Centro Cultural Mexicano, 16300 Redmond Way Ste 100  
Listed with Welcoming Week partners.  
[Welcoming Week](https://www.redmond.gov/1254/Welcoming-Week)

**Columbia Choirs Community Sing** · Sun Sep 20 · 12:30–1:30 p.m.  
Redmond Library, 15990 NE 85th St · Free, all-ages, cross-cultural sing-along (KCLS partnership).

**Washington Ganesh Festival** · Fri–Sun Sep 18–20 · Marymoor Park  
Organizer (Beats of Washington / Beats of Redmond) listed a free community festival at King County’s Marymoor Park. Confirm hours and parking with the organizer before you go.  
[beatsofwa.org](https://beatsofwa.org/events/ganesh-festival-redmond-2026) · [Zeffy listing](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/ticketing/washington-ganesh-festival--2026)
