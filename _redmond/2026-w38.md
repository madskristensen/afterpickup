## Coming up that week

**Downtown Redmond Art Walk (DRAW)**
Thursday, Sept. 17, 4 to 7 p.m. starting at Downtown Park and participating businesses. Free evening of local art and merchants. [DRAW details](https://www.redmond.gov/1967/Downtown-Redmond-Art-Walk) · [redmond.gov/DRAW](https://www.redmond.gov/DRAW)

**Budget public hearing**
Tuesday, Sept. 15, 7 p.m. City Council business meeting (one of four hearings for the 2027–2028 budget). Watch at [redmond.gov/rctvlive](https://www.redmond.gov/rctvlive) or email [budget@redmond.gov](mailto:budget@redmond.gov). [Budget engagement](https://www.redmond.gov/BudgetEngagement)

**Youth Climate Action Fund applications**
Deadline Sunday, Sept. 20. Teams ages 15–24 could apply for $1,000–$5,000 microgrants for Redmond climate projects. Fair and office hours ran earlier in September at Marymoor Village and elsewhere. Check [NewsFlash](https://www.redmond.gov/m/NewsFlash/) sustainability notes for follow-up.

**Volunteer event at Heron Rookery Park**
Friday, Sept. 19 window on the city calendar (and earlier Sept. 4 / 11 slots). Good short outdoor volunteer option for older kids with an adult. [City calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx)
