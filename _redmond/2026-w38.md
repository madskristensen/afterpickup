Thursday is Downtown Redmond Art Walk, in the park in the photo. The same stretch of days is the last half of Welcoming Week, plus the first public hearing on the 2027-2028 budget.

## Downtown Redmond Art Walk

Thursday, Sept. 17. The city activates Downtown Park from 4 to 7 p.m. with art and performances, and publishes a map of participating downtown businesses. It is the fourth annual walk, pairing those businesses with local artists. [Art Walk page](https://www.redmond.gov/1967/Downtown-Redmond-Art-Walk) and [calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3535). The park photo is from the city's [Downtown Park](https://www.redmond.gov/955/Downtown-Park) page.

## Welcoming Week

Redmond's Welcoming Week runs Sept. 10 to 19. What still falls in this week:

- Wednesday, Sept. 16, 5 to 7 p.m., Stories We Share: The Courage to Connect, at The Together Center, 16305 NE 87th St.
- Thursday, Sept. 17, the Art Walk above.
- Friday, Sept. 18, 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., Hispanic Heritage Month Art Exhibition at Centro Cultural Mexicano, 16300 Redmond Way Ste 100.
- Saturday, Sept. 19, 4 to 8 p.m., Arts in the Park at Downtown Park. The city lists this for each Saturday in September.
- Sunday, Sept. 20, 12:30 to 1:30 p.m., Columbia Choirs community sing at Redmond Library, 15990 NE 85th St.
- Sunday, Sept. 20, 1 to 5 p.m., Eastside Immigrant Youth Advocacy Day at The Together Center, with Africans on the Eastside, The Circle, and Eastside For All.

Details are on the city's [Welcoming Week](https://www.redmond.gov/1254/Welcoming-Week) page.

## Budget hearing

Tuesday, Sept. 15, at 7 p.m., the first of four public hearings on the 2027-2028 budget, during the City Council business meeting at City Hall. Later hearings are Oct. 6, Oct. 20, and Nov. 2. People can attend in person, watch on [redmond.gov/rctvlive](https://www.redmond.gov/rctvlive), or listen at 510-335-7371. The online budget questionnaire closed Sept. 11. [Budget engagement](https://www.redmond.gov/2622/Budget-Engagement) and the [news release](https://www.redmond.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/3060).

The most recent Council Conversation on the city's page was already held: Thursday, Sept. 10, 5 to 7 p.m., at Spiritbrook Park, with the 2027-2028 budget as the topic. [Council Conversations](https://www.redmond.gov/2238/Council-Conversations).

## Youth climate deadline

Applications for the Youth Climate Action Fund are due Sunday, Sept. 20. The city describes grants of $1,000 to $5,000 for youth teams ages 15 to 24. Office hours for people working on an application start at 3 p.m. on Thursday, Sept. 17, by 15-minute appointment. The calendar listing does not name a room. [Calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3549), [program page](https://www.redmond.gov/2586/Youth-Climate-Action-Fund), and [application form](https://www.redmond.gov/FormCenter/Executive-17/Youth-Climate-Action-Fund-Application-294).

## Salary commission

On Sept. 14 the city asked for applicants for two volunteer Salary Commission seats. Residents of Redmond who also live in the 98052 zip code can apply. Details are on the [week of September 21](/redmond/2026/w39/) page, and in the [news release](https://www.redmond.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/3066).
