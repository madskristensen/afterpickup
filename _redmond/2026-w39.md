Saturday is the one to circle if you have a teen: the Back to School Bash at the Teen Lounge. Earlier in the week, the city is pointing homeowners at a free heat pump webinar.

## Teen Back to School Bash

Saturday, Sept. 26, starting at noon at the Teen Lounge in the Redmond Community Center at Marymoor Village (the building in the photo). The calendar describes it as a kickoff for the school year: meet people, see the space, hear about programs, and stay for games, music, and food. New and returning teens are both welcome. [Calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3536).

The same city page that lists Welcoming Week events also lists Arts in the Park for each Saturday in September, 4 to 8 p.m. at Downtown Park. That includes this Saturday. [Welcoming Week](https://www.redmond.gov/1254/Welcoming-Week).

## Heat pump webinar

Tuesday, Sept. 22, 7 to 8 p.m., online and free. Energy Smart Eastside staff cover how heat pumps work, rebates, and how to find an installer. The September sustainability newsletter is the source, and registration is on the city's [sign-up form](https://share-na2.hsforms.com/1lV2ixsFhQpG6vUgW5cnsjgrh66b). [Newsletter](https://www.redmond.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/3050).

## Salary commission

The city is filling two volunteer seats on the Redmond Salary Commission. Applicants need to live in Redmond and in the 98052 zip code. Residency has to be verified by 5 p.m. on Oct. 16. There is no application deadline, and an initial screening is planned by Oct. 19. [News release](https://www.redmond.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/3066) and [Salary Commission](https://www.redmond.gov/1972/Salary-Commission).

## Salmon

On Sept. 16 the city posted a "Redmond in the News" item, updated Sept. 18, pointing to coverage of salmon returning to Redmond streams. [City item](https://www.redmond.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/3075) and the [Hoodline story](https://hoodline.com/2026/09/salmon-return-to-redmond-streams-offering-public-a-front-row-view-of-migration/) it links.

## Already past, still useful

The Downtown Redmond Art Walk was Thursday, Sept. 17, with Downtown Park active from 4 to 7 p.m. The city page now has the artist list and the map of participating businesses. [Art Walk](https://www.redmond.gov/1967/Downtown-Redmond-Art-Walk).

The first 2027-2028 budget hearing was Tuesday, Sept. 15. The next hearing is Tuesday, Oct. 6, at 7 p.m. [Budget engagement](https://www.redmond.gov/2622/Budget-Engagement).
