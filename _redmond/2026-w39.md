A Saturday packed with teens, BBQ, crafts, market finds, and a free evening concert in Downtown Park.

## For teens
**Teen Back to School Bash** · Sat Sep 26 · 12:00–3:00 p.m.  
Redmond Community Center at Marymoor Village, 6505 176th Ave NE  
City-listed community event for teens.  
[City calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3536)

## Parks, market & BBQ
**Redmond Saturday Market** · Sat Sep 26 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.  
9900 Willows Rd NE · Open through Oct 31.  
[redmondsaturdaymarket.org](http://www.redmondsaturdaymarket.org/)

**Smoke on the Sound** · Sat Sep 26 · 11:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. (public day; competition also Sun Sep 27)  
Marymoor Park, 6046 W Lake Sammamish Pkwy NE  
Inaugural KCBS BBQ competition. OneRedmond and the organizer list free public attendance Saturday with tasting, music, and a beer garden vibe. Family note: beer garden on site.  
[OneRedmond](https://engage.oneredmond.org/events/smoke-on-the-sound) · [smokeonthesound.com](https://www.smokeonthesound.com/)

**Arts in the Park** · Sat Sep 26 · 4:00–8:00 p.m.  
Downtown Park, 16101 Redmond Way · Free. Redmond Academy of Theatre Arts, Seattle Community Theater, Faizi Piano Academy students & professionals, plus Eastside Made artisans.  
[OneRedmond](https://engage.oneredmond.org/events/arts-in-the-park) · [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/arts-in-the-park-redmond-tickets-1996026726671)
