## Coming up that week

**Free heat pump webinar**
Tuesday, Sept. 22, 7 to 8 p.m. Energy Smart Eastside virtual workshop on heat pumps, rebates, and installers. Watch for recordings via city sustainability / [NewsFlash](https://www.redmond.gov/m/NewsFlash/).

**Teen Back to School Bash**
Saturday, Sept. 26, noon to 3 p.m. at the Redmond Community Center at Marymoor Village. Free afternoon for teens. [City calendar](https://www.redmond.gov/Calendar.aspx)

**City Council study session**
Tuesday, Sept. 22 evening (committee of the whole earlier the same day). Agendas on [Legistar](https://redmond.legistar.com/) and remote viewing at [redmond.gov/rctvlive](https://www.redmond.gov/rctvlive).

## City news worth knowing

**Volunteer Salary Commissioners**
Two seats open for 98052 residents. Residency verification by 5 p.m. Oct. 16; initial screening by Oct. 19; confirmation targeted for Nov. 17. Apply: [redmond.gov/SalaryCommission](https://www.redmond.gov/SalaryCommission)

**Teen Center Project engagement starts next week**
Drop-ins for teens and the community begin Sept. 30. Preview: [redmond.gov/TeenCenterProject](https://www.redmond.gov/TeenCenterProject)

**Salmon in Redmond streams**
Mid-September coverage noted salmon returning with public viewing opportunities. Nice weekend walk material. See NewsFlash “Redmond in the News.”

**RedLink shuttle**
Recent expansion of the electric shuttle service area. Useful for car-free hops around town; details via [redmond.gov](https://www.redmond.gov/) transit updates.

**Budget hearings continue**
Next open hearings after Sept. 15: Oct. 6, Oct. 20, and Nov. 2 at 7 p.m. [Budget engagement](https://www.redmond.gov/BudgetEngagement)
