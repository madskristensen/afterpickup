Each of our three kids has a [plastic banker box with hanging file folders](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09FVM5RNL/ref=nosim?tag=afterpickup-20){:rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank"}, and that's where we keep their important papers and the things we want to save. Birth certificates and immunization records go in there, along with the rest of the official paperwork, and so do their baby books, drawings they've done, and the other cute little things they've made over the years. Class photos and stuff like that are saved too.

What's really nice is that everything for each kid is contained in one box. We can stack them out in the garage, where they're out of the way, but we still have everything we need when we need it, and they're easy to get to.

They come home every day and every week from school with a bunch of artwork and schoolwork and notes from teachers. We take the best stuff they bring home, and the cool things they're doing at home, and put it into the file for that grade. We also clean up, so we just keep the best stuff. Because they're the filing cabinet type, it's easy to flip through the hanging folders and find the right category of things.

The lid actually doubles. It has little compartments, so we put little keepsakes up there, things they've done through art and crafts.

It's a nice way of keeping everything, but we also go through it every now and again to make sure only the good stuff stays. The idea is that when they turn 18, we're going to give them one file box that has every year in their life up to that point.
