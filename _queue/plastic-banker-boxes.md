Each of our three kids has a [plastic banker box with hanging file folders](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09FVM5RNL/ref=nosim?tag=afterpickup-20){:rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank"}, and that's where we keep their important papers and the things we want to save. Birth certificates and immunization records go in there, along with the rest of the official paperwork, and so do their baby books, drawings they've done, and the other cute little things they've made over the years.

What's really nice is that everything for each kid is contained in one box. We can stack them out in the garage, where they're out of the way, but we still have everything we need when we need it, and they're easy to get to.

Because they're the filing cabinet type, it's easy to flip through the hanging folders and find the right category of things, whether we're looking for a record or something we saved. There's a file for each grade level as well, to keep some of the cool things they're doing that year in school or at home, art and homework and things we want to keep.

It's a simple setup, and it's super handy. When we need a record, or we want to look back at something they made, we just get that kid's box and flip to it.
