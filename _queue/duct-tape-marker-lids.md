When the kids sit down at a restaurant with a coloring page, our markers come out as one piece, because we duct tape the marker lids together into a single bundle. Each kid pulls out the color they want, the lid stays behind in the taped row, and when they are done the marker goes right back into its spot, which is exactly what is happening with the pink one in the photo.

Making one takes just a few minutes. You take a set of your favorite washable markers, line them up with the lids on, and run duct tape across the lids so they all become one unit. There is nothing special to buy, and chances are you already have both the markers and the duct tape somewhere at home.

That bundle is the whole drawing kit when we head out. We bring that one thing and we have all the colors, and at the table the kids can easily grab the marker they want without anything rolling away. At family restaurants the crayons they hand out always seem to end up on the floor, and these just do not.

Airplanes are where it really pays off. The tray table is tiny, and loose pens, crayons, and pencils always find their way to the floor, which means struggling to find them and sometimes unbuckling and getting out of your seat just to reach one, and that gets old fast. With the lids taped together the markers stay put on the tray, and any marker that is not in someone's hand is already back in the bundle.

It has become one of those small things we do not think about anymore. The markers come from [the craft closet](/craft-closet/), a strip of duct tape turns them into a [travel set](/travel-toys/), and the kids get to color wherever we are going while we stay in our seats.
