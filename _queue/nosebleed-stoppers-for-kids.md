When Mads was a kid he got nosebleeds all the time, and the fix back then was whatever was closest, a wad of tissue or a folded paper towel held up to his nose until it stopped. That works, and it still works, but now that two of our kids sometimes get nosebleeds too, what we reach for first is a little pack of nosebleed stoppers for kids that we bought on Amazon.

The idea actually came from one of our boys, because nosebleed stoppers are what they use at school, and once we had a pack at home it was easy to see why. What a bloody nose really needs is calm and for nobody to touch it, and a stopper gives you both beautifully. They are small and cheap, and they fit a child's nostril pretty perfectly. In our experience they help the bleeding stop, and they stay where you put them, so you just pop one in and that is it. They are not the kind of thing that can get stuck, so they feel safe to us in that regard, and they do not fall out when a kid turns their head or gets up to come find one of us.

The tissue has one weak spot with kids, which is that they forget to hold it. A few minutes in, the hand drifts down, the tissue ends up in their lap, and the blood drips onto the bed or the front of a shirt. With a stopper in, there is nothing for them to remember to hold, so the sheets and their clothes stay a lot cleaner and nobody has to stand there reminding them.

They have come in handy in more places than our own house, too. On one flight, another family's kid got a nosebleed, and Caitlin handed over one of our stoppers. The parents looked at her as if she had magical powers, wondering what this magic thing was, and they were very grateful. That is a big part of why we like having a few with us when we travel, because a nosebleed does not wait for a convenient moment.

They are a small thing, and cheap enough that we never think twice about using one, but they take a lot of the scramble out of a nosebleed. If nosebleeds come often or are heavy, that is worth a conversation with your pediatrician, and for the everyday kind at our house, a stopper and a few calm minutes is what has worked for us.
