We sit on a wooden barstool while Caitlin cuts our hair at home, and when she is done we just vacuum around it. It is very simple, and the barstool in the photo is the one we actually sit on, with the clipper, the scissors, a comb, and a neck brush on the seat.

That routine started during COVID, when Caitlin first began cutting hair for Mads and the kids, and then for her mom too. It took a lot of trial and error. She was terrified the first time she tried to cut Mads's hair, and it did not turn out well, so he had to go to the hairdresser and have it fixed. That was actually a mechanical issue. Mads has short hair, and we tried to use his beard trimmer for the edging and the neck, which was the wrong tool, and it did not go so well.

She has been cutting our hair ever since, and the thing that finally made it work, for the adults and the kids in the house, was getting a few pieces of equipment that are actually meant for this. First is a proper trimmer, one you plug in. They are not very expensive, and they are so much better than a beard trimmer or whatever else you already have around. They are purpose built for cutting hair, they go through it very easily, and it is very easy to avoid mistakes with a tool like that.

The other thing was a couple of proper scissors. One is a regular pair of hairdresser scissors. Caitlin is left-handed, and left-handed ones are, weirdly enough, more expensive, though they are still not that expensive, and that was helpful. The other pair has teeth, and those are really good for bangs and for when you want natural looking edges, so the hair does not look so boxy and weird.

Those few simple tools are all we need, and she has been cutting our hair for years now, and it is going really well. We sit on the barstool, she cuts, and we vacuum around it afterwards.
