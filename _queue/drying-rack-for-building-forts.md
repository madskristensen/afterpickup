The kids love building forts, and they do it often enough that we keep the sheets and clamps ready in our [fort-building bin](/fort-building-bin/). When it's time to put a fort up, we set out a [drying rack](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DQ3RR8TY/ref=nosim?tag=afterpickup-20){:rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank"} for them to clamp those sheets onto, the kind that stands on its feet and folds out a little.

We often used chairs for that, and cushions too, but the cushions fall down or they're not very tall, and the chairs aren't always the best thing to attach the blankets or sheets to. The drying rack is relatively tall and lightweight, so the kids can move it around themselves, and it's strong enough to hold a lot of weight, including the blankets they put on top.

It's easy for them to work with too. They can shift it where they want it and clamp the sheets from the bin right onto it, and the height of the rack is enough to hold those sheets and blankets up while they keep building.

The bin gives them the sheets and the clamps, and the drying rack is the piece they clamp them to, something tall and light enough that they can move it around as the fort takes shape.
