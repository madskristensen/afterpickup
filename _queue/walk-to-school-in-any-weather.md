The kids always walk to school, no matter the weather, and we make sure there are umbrellas for everybody and that they know how to use them, so there's never an excuse to drive just because it's raining.

Those umbrellas live in a bin right outside the front door, where they're super easy to grab on the way out, and the coats and backpacks have their own place [in the cubbies just inside](/kids-entry-cubbies/). The kids often want to keep an umbrella at school with them and then use it again on the way back.

Once they reach first or second grade, they walk to school and leave school on their own. There's still no excuse to drive, rain or not, because the umbrellas are already out there and they already know what to do with them.

In the darker months, when it's dark on the way home, we make sure they have [little stick-on reflective patches](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CXK93JW5/ref=nosim?tag=afterpickup-20){:rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank"} on their jackets and raincoats.
