There are often [art projects](/craft-mats/) on the table, or other things left there, and when the kids help set the table they start by getting those out of the way so it's ready for dinner. They've been doing that since they were four.

We set the plates, the utensils, and the sauces from the fridge, like ketchup, out on the kitchen counter, and the kids come pick them up and put them on the table. They get the [cloth napkins](/black-bar-towels/) from the drawer too, and they already know how those go, with the knife and fork on top of the napkin next to each plate.

As they get older, they can handle the warm dishes we set out on the counter, like rice or pasta, and they put trivets underneath. We teach them how to carry something warm and keep it balanced, and to watch for anyone coming the other way, especially if they're walking with a heavy pot of food.

Afterwards they all help clear their own plates. At four or five that means taking their own plate to the sink, and as they get older we tell them not to stop moving things off the table over to the kitchen sink until the table is clear. One of them takes the cloth napkins to the washing machine, and that's one of the more popular parts of clearing the table among the kids. We keep giving them more of the job as they get older.
