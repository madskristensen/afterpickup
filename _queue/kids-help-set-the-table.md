The kids have been helping set the table since they were four, and they start by clearing it. There are often [art projects](/craft-mats/) on the table, or other things left there, and they get those out of the way so it's ready for dinner.

It's easy enough for us to set the plates, the utensils, and the sauces from the fridge, like ketchup, out on the kitchen counter, and then the kids come pick them up and put them on the table. They get the [cloth napkins](/black-bar-towels/) from the drawer too, and they already know how those go. The plates go down, and the knife and fork sit on top of the napkin next to each plate.

As they get older, they can handle the warm dishes we set out on the counter, like rice or pasta, and they put trivets underneath. We teach them how to carry something warm and keep it balanced, and to look so nobody's coming who might bump into them while they're walking with a heavy pot of food.

Afterwards they all help clear their own plates. At four or five that means taking their own plate to the sink, and as they get older we tell them not to stop moving things off the table over to the kitchen sink until the table is clear. They all take the cloth napkins to the washing machine too. We keep giving them more of the job as they get older, the way we have since they were four.
