Last year, when our oldest turned ten, we skipped the bouncy house, the arcade, and the trampoline park and threw him a cheap 10 year old birthday party at the Pokemon card and game store in town instead. We invited about eight of his friends, and it turned out that a minivan and an SUV were all we needed to fit every kid, so the whole party piled into two cars and headed out together.

The drive ended up being an event of its own. The kids took turns requesting music, phones got plugged into the car speakers, and the silly songs kept coming, so there was laughing the whole way there and the whole way back. We also had walkie-talkies passing messages between the two cars, and while they only worked about half the time, the kids got a real kick out of them anyway.

At the store, we gave each kid ten dollars to buy a card, or anything else they wanted, that they could keep and take home. Not everybody went for Pokemon, since the shop had other kinds of cards and toys too, and that was part of what made it feel like their own pick. We were there about 45 minutes, and they really took their time, flipping through binders, comparing choices with each other, and changing their minds before settling on something.

Back home we had Costco pizza, and for the kids whose parents were running late for pickup, the party wrapped up with an impromptu Nerf war. Many hands make light work, so cleanup was easy, because each kid had to pick up 20 Nerf bullets before heading out the door.

Everyone was happy, and it was one of the most fun birthdays we have done and also one of the cheapest. A bouncy house runs roughly $500 to $700, so if you are looking for budget birthday party ideas that skip the bouncy house, this one cost us a lot less, and every kid came home with something they had picked out themselves.
