The kids always like butter pasta, and they've started mixing in other things we set out with it on the table, then they name the dishes they make.

We want them to have some protein too, and one night they took their butter spaghetti and added chicken nuggets we'd made in the oven and chopped up, then they put ketchup on and used it to dip the nuggets. They named it Chispetti, a mix of chicken and spaghetti, and it was a huge success.

It doesn't have to be chicken nuggets, either. It could be hot dogs, other types of meat, or vegetables added to the spaghetti, or to rice, and the kids can make up a funny name for that too.

They didn't like omelets, and we couldn't get them to eat one, even though we really wanted them eating more eggs for the nutrition. So we made an omelet and cut it into triangular wedges, like pizza, and they named it Egg pizza. They've loved it ever since, even though it's literally just an omelet with a different name.

Our kids are like anyone else, in that what tastes good today doesn't taste good tomorrow, and you buy the 12-pack or the 20-pack of the thing they finally like, only for them to stop liking it right after. What we've found is that if you let them [mix and match their own dishes from the ingredients we put on the table](/ground-beef-dinner-bowls/), and then name them, it makes it a lot more fun, and they eat more of it that way.
