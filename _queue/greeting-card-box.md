Years ago Caitlin bought a box of cards with a slot for every occasion, birthdays, anniversaries, the usual ones, and it has sat on the shelf in this house ever since. The box is how we keep a card ready, instead of hunting the night before.

When she sees a card she likes at the store, she buys it to restock the box, and that is how the dividers stay filled. A card comes home with her when it looks right, and the stash is waiting the next time we need one.

That habit has saved us more than once. We have headed to a family dinner and only realized it was somebody's anniversary moments before we left, and the box was still on the shelf, so we pulled a card and walked out the door.

The kids have thought they had a Mother's Day card ready and then remembered they left it at school on the morning of the big day. The box covered that too. Someone could still write in a card at home, and we were not starting the morning with a store run.

It is not fancy, just a deep enough stash that the house can answer the calendar without a last-minute trip. Caitlin keeps the dividers filled, and we stop treating greeting cards like an errand we always forget until it is too late.
