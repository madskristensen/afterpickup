On a lot of mornings Mads heads out the door with a cold brew in a clear plastic cup, the lid pressed on snug, much like the cold brew cups a coffee shop hands you across the counter. He drinks it on the way and through the morning at work, and when it is empty he simply throws it out right there, so nothing comes back home with him at the end of the day and nothing is waiting by the sink.

The cups come from Amazon in a big pack of 16-ounce clear plastic cups with matching lids, and we keep the pack at home so there is always one ready whenever somebody is heading out with something cold. They are the plain disposable kind, nothing special about them, which is exactly why they work so well for us.

We reach for one whenever we are taking iced coffee or a cold drink somewhere and do not want to bring a thermos or a travel mug that we would have to carry home again. A thermos is lovely right up until it is empty, and then it is a metal cup you are holding for the rest of the outing. The mall is a good example, because once the drink is gone there is no easy place to put a thermos away, so it ends up riding along in your hand from store to store, while a plastic cup goes in the first trash can we pass and our hands are free again.

We still make the coffee the way we always do, [pouring the cream in between two pours of cold brew](/pour-the-cream-in-the-middle/) so it mixes without a spoon, only now it goes into a cup with a lid instead of a glass. That keeps the whole routine about as light as it gets, with no spoon in the sink and no thermos or travel mug to wash at night, and no lid to take apart and dry before the next morning.

It is a small thing to keep a pack of cups and lids in the house, but it has made taking a cold drink along feel easy. If it is iced and it is leaving the house, it usually leaves in one of these, and it does not need to come back.
