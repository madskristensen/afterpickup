We pack small water bottles that fit inside the lunch box, with enough water just for lunch sitting easily alongside the food. The bottle and the box leave the house together, and that is what comes back at the end of the day.

At school, lunchboxes and water bottles are trolleyed to the lunch room, and after lunch the kids are responsible for putting the lunch box and the water bottle back on the trolley. When the bottle lives inside the box, it goes back on the trolley with the lunch, so the water comes home instead of getting left behind.

A full water bottle is heavy and cumbersome to move around, and every so often the kids would forget it and only realize once they were home. The small bottles stay with the food, so there is nothing heavy to carry through the rest of the day and nothing separate to remember on the way back to the trolley.

All the kids love it, and they think it is weird that other parents do not do the same, because they see other kids forgetting bottles and dealing with them every day. It is a small change, but it improved their lunch time.
